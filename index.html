Learning Objectives

What You Should
Achieve

After studying the six lessons, learners should be able to demonstrate the following knowledge and skills.

1

Describe the meaning of animation and explain major developments from early optical toys to digital and real-time animation.

2

Explain the purpose, brand identity, production roles, and ethical responsibilities of an animation studio.

3

Identify visual elements and principles of design and explain how they guide effective visual communication.

4

Recognize color groups, color schemes, typeface categories, and typography spacing used in design.

5

Compare pixels, resolution, raster and vector graphics, and common image file formats.

6

Explain the purpose of storyboards, framing, and camera shots when planning a visual sequence.
